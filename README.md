# Shubham Vyas

![Profile Views](https://komarev.com/ghpvc/?username=profvyas&color=0ea5e9&style=flat-square&label=Profile+Views)

**Founder building AI-native operating systems for commerce, supply chain, and warehouse teams.**  
Currently building **FilFlo** from Delhi, India.

I work at the messy intersection of e-commerce operations, logistics, inventory truth, and applied AI. My current focus is turning fragmented seller workflows into intelligent systems that can observe, reason, and act across orders, inventory, invoicing, procurement, and dispatch.

## Building Now

| System | What I am building |
| --- | --- |
| **FilFlo OS** | A supply-chain observability and execution platform for e-commerce and B2B teams. |
| **AI ops layer** | Chat, voice, and agentic workflows for operational decisions, exception handling, and internal copilots. |
| **Warehouse workflows** | Inventory inward, FIFO rack allocation, picklists, dispatch scans, inventory ledgers, adjustments, GRNs, and B2B purchase flows. |
| **Commerce integrations** | Platform workflows around Flipkart, Blinkit, Shopify-style demand planning, ASN settings, and marketplace ops. |
| **Finance automation** | Invoice flows, e-invoice and e-way bill services, GST-aware documents, audit logs, and PDF/QR generation. |
| **Production ops** | Job orders, BOMs, procurement, supplier debit notes, and operational controls for growing inventory-heavy businesses. |
| **Deployment muscle** | DigitalOcean, PM2, Nginx, MongoDB, Redis, health checks, and production release workflows. |
| **Writing** | Notes on systems integration, operations frameworks, supply-chain lessons, and founder thinking at [profvyas.com](https://profvyas.com). |

## AI-Native Direction

- Building products where AI is part of the workflow, not a bolt-on chat box.
- Designing operational agents that sit close to source-of-truth data.
- Using structured schemas, event trails, and audit logs so AI outputs can be verified.
- Exploring voice interfaces, collaborative workspaces, AI SDKs, and assistant-style UX for teams that live inside dashboards all day.
- Turning repetitive ops work into reviewable suggestions, automations, and exception queues.
