FilFlo

Quick commerce rewards brands that replenish with speed, precision, and operational memory. FilFlo is built for that world: short cycles, volatile demand, channel-specific inventory, and teams that cannot wait for ERP-heavy workflows to catch up.

  • Autonomous replenishment for Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, and the operating loops around them.
  • Inventory truth across stock, velocity, fill-rate risk, procurement, and dispatch readiness.
  • Anti-ERP workflow that gives operators decisions and actions, not another system to babysit.
